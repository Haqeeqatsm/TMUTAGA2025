TAGA’s one and only Canadian chapter since 2006.

TMUTAGA is a team of students from the Graphic Communications Management (GCM) program at Toronto Metropolitan University. We create and produce a journal that highlights our team's creative and technical skills while showcasing thesis research papers from our very own graduating class.
